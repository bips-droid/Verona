VERONA

A city written in love.

Ancient stones, candlelit evenings, river bridges and the timeless story of Romeo and Juliet.

Why Verona
feels different

Verona is the kind of city that rewards slow travel. Its Roman arena, medieval streets, river bridges and intimate piazzas are close enough to explore on foot, yet every turn feels like a new scene.

The Adige curls around the historic centre, while warm stone façades catch the evening light. It is beautiful without needing to hurry - and that makes it perfect for a romantic escape.

The balcony, the legend, the feeling

Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet made Verona famous as a city of star-crossed lovers. Casa di giulietta is associated with the Capulet story, and its balcony became one of the world's most recognizable symbols of romantic Verona.

But the city's romance does not depend on believing every detail of the legend. The magic comes from the way literature, architecture and imagination have become woven into Verona's identity.

Life here is meant to be savoured

The Passeggiata

In the early evening, Italians often stroll, meet friends and linger in public spaces. In Verona, this slow rhythm is part of the charm.

Piazza Life

Piazza delle Erbe is a natural centre for people-watching, market browsing, coffee and an unhurried walk through the historic heart.

Opera & Arena

The Arena di Verona connects the city to ancient Rome and to its living performance culture. Opera beneath the night sky turns history into an experience.

Local Warmth

Take time for a long meal, greet people politely, and allow the city to unfold rather than rushing from landmark to landmark.

A table made for two

Veronese food is generous, regional and made for lingering. Build this part of the blog like a little menu:one dish, one memory, one reason to stay for dessert.

Risotto all’Amarone

Creamy risotto enriched with Amarone wine - one of Verona's signature combinations.

Gnocchi

Soft potato dumplings with many regional variations - comforting and unmistakably Italian.

Pandoro

Verona's famous star-shaped Christmas cake, light and buttery, finished with powdered sugar.
